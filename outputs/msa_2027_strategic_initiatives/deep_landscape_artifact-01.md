| **pyxem** | Electron-diffraction and 4D-STEM analysis | Diffraction-data processing, virtual imaging, orientation/crystallographic analysis, and scalable lazy computation through the HyperSpy/Dask ecosystem (pqac-00000000) | Does not provide cross-modal SEM/EDS/EBSD/XRF acquisition planning, literature-grounded justification, standards-rationale capture, or an experimental feedback loop that learns calibrated recommendation uncertainty |
| **py4DSTEM** | Analysis of four-dimensional STEM datasets | Open-source calibration, diffraction analysis, property measurement, visualization, and HDF5-based data handling for large 4D-STEM datasets | Specialized for 4D-STEM analysis rather than SEM/EDS/EBSD/XRF parameter selection. | **EMsoft** | Physics-based electron-scattering, diffraction, imaging, and EBSD simulation | Dynamical forward simulation and master-pattern generation; supplies synthetic reference patterns for dictionary indexing, ML training, and validation | A forward-simulation suite, not a community recommender. | **DREAM.3D** | Three-dimensional microstructure reconstruction, characterization, and synthetic-volume generation | Pipeline-based segmentation and statistics; construction of representative volume elements; processing of EBSD-derived microstructures; integration with modeling and cloud workflows | Operates mainly downstream of acquisition. | **pycroscopy / pyUSID** | Standardized storage, provenance, and analysis of imaging and spectroscopic data | USID/HDF5 data model; translators; scalable analysis; links source data, metadata, and derived results to improve traceability and reproducibility (pqac-00000001, pqac-00000006) | Captures data and processing provenance better than most analysis packages, but not the complete upstream decision trace: why an acquisition was configured a certain way, what alternatives failed, or how literature and standards justify a recommendation. Cross-file provenance was also identified as incomplete (pqac-00000001) |
| **AtomAI** | Deep-learning analysis of microscopy images and spectra | Segmentation, atomic-coordinate extraction, invariant representation learning, image–spectrum relationships, and links to first-principles modeling | Primarily interprets acquired data. | **ImageJ / Fiji** | General-purpose image processing and scientific-image analysis | Mature GUI; extensive plugin and macro ecosystem; segmentation, measurement, filtering, registration, and reproducible scripted workflows across many image types | Broad image analysis but limited native representation of materials-microscopy acquisition context. It does not recommend SEM/EDS/EBSD/XRF settings or preserve literature evidence, standards rationale, adaptive decision histories, and calibrated confidence |
| **napari** | Interactive, extensible visualization of multidimensional images | Python-based n-dimensional viewer; layers, annotations, plugins, and interactive integration with analysis and ML workflows | A visualization and plugin host rather than an acquisition-knowledge system. Any recommendation, literature retrieval, metadata validation, rationale capture, or uncertainty feedback must be supplied by external plugins and infrastructure |
| **OME-Zarr** | Cloud-native storage and exchange of large multidimensional bioimaging data | Chunked, compressed, multiscale arrays; parallel and remote access; interoperable visualization and analysis; OME-associated image metadata | Strong for scalable pixel/array transport, but OME semantics are predominantly oriented toward biological optical microscopy rather than materials microanalysis (pqac-00000047). It neither recommends parameters nor natively represents the reasoning, physics, standards clauses, failed settings, and uncertainty behind SEM/EDS/EBSD/XRF choices |
| **NeXus / FAIRmat NXem** | Hierarchical, schema-validated representation of electron-microscopy data and metadata | NXem can organize experiments, instruments, samples, hardware, beam generation, images, spectra, acquisition settings, time-dependent metadata, and provenance for SEM/TEM/STEM workflows (pqac-00000045, pqac-00000049) | Provides a promising storage target, not a recommender or knowledge base. It does not itself infer settings or capture tacit rationale. Coverage and terminology remain incomplete for some materials-microscopy uses; reported schema gaps include fields such as magnification, working distance, pixel size, stage tilt, and tilt compensation in particular implementations (pqac-00000047, pqac-00000048) |
| **Collective landscape** | Complementary ecosystem spanning simulation, acquisition-data representation, analysis, reconstruction, ML, and visualization | Existing projects supply many components CALIBER should reuse rather than duplicate: scalable arrays, translators, schemas, provenance, domain algorithms, forward models, viewers, and plugin systems | No listed resource unifies **literature/standards retrieval + structured experimental data + multi-modal SEM/EDS/EBSD/XRF parameter recommendation + calibrated uncertainty/abstention + closed-loop learning + preservation of procedural and decision rationale**. The broader ecosystem remains fragmented, with limited benchmarks, integration strategies, instrument APIs, and upstream experiment planning (pqac-00000002, pqac-00000003, pqac-00000020) |
